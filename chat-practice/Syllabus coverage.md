# Syllabus coverage for chat practice

Edition used: Cambridge O Level Pakistan Studies 2059, examination in 2027. Counts assign each question to one primary area. History numbering follows the official key questions; Geography numbering follows the eight Paper 2 topics. [Official syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/732849-2027-syllabus.pdf)

The 5,600 cards sample knowledge and reasoning across the syllabus. Some detailed case examples, named places and individual content bullets still lack a dedicated question. The scores measure performance on the sampled questions. Difficulty has yet to be calibrated with learners.

## Geography subtopics

Location and coordinates, administrative areas and cities, landforms and drainage, and climate appear in area 1. Area 2 includes water, forests, minerals and fish. Area 3 covers energy sources, non-renewables and renewables. Area 4 covers farming systems, crops and livestock, and factors affecting production. Area 5 covers industrial terms and secondary and tertiary activity. Area 6 covers imports, exports and trading partners. Area 7 covers internal and international transport and telecommunications. Area 8 covers population structure and growth, migration, density and distribution, and employment.
